About Digital Dark Spot

AI-assisted information gap detection. It reads the sources you give it and tells you what they do not say, where they disagree, and what to ask next.

The problem

Notices, offers and guides often leave out the deadline, the fee or the catch. Readers find out after they have already acted.

How it works

Read

Splits each source into sentences.

Match

Checks six topics: deadline, fees, eligibility, how to apply, contact, terms.

Compare

Flags different dates or amounts across sources.

Ask

Turns every gap into a question to verify.

Tech and privacy

Front end

HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step.

Private by default

Analysis runs in your browser. Text is only sent out if you use URL fetch, PDF/OCR libraries or the optional AI box.

Installable

Works as an app and offline after the first visit.
